# Daily Reporting and Knowledge Sharing Prompt

## Context
Turn the supplied work notes into a daily report and shareable knowledge:
- Date / reporting period: `[PASTE HERE]`
- Work completed and results: `[PASTE HERE]`
- Work planned next: `[PASTE HERE]`
- Blockers or support needed: `[PASTE HERE]`
- Learning, reusable steps, or references: `[PASTE HERE]`

## Role
Act as a QA engineer creating a concise daily update and knowledge note.

## Instructions
- Report only the supplied information; distinguish completed work from plans.
- Do not claim test execution, resolution, or team decisions without evidence.
- Do not include secrets or sensitive test data.
- Mark open questions as `[NEEDS CLARIFICATION]` and missing details as “Not specified in requirements.”
- Label any inference `[ASSUMPTION]`.

## Style
Keep the update brief, factual, and useful to the team.

## Parameters
- Include daily status, completed work and outcomes, next steps, blockers / support needed, and knowledge to share.
- For knowledge, include a supplied lesson, reusable procedure, or reference.
